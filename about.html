About Me

Hello! I'm Mfundo Percy Zwane, a Software Developer and Networking Enthusiast with a passion for building practical software solutions that solve real-world problems. I enjoy transforming ideas into applications that are useful, reliable, and easy to use.

My journey into technology began with a qualification as an electrician, where I developed discipline, analytical thinking, attention to detail, and strong problem-solving skills. Those experiences continue to influence the way I approach software development today.

While I value my electrical background, I became increasingly fascinated by the world of software and technology. What excites me most about Information Technology is its limitless opportunities for learning, innovation, and continuous growth. I enjoy exploring new technologies, improving my skills, and challenging myself to build solutions that make a positive impact.

Today, I am expanding my knowledge in Java, Android development, SQL, web development, and computer networking. I am currently developing projects such as Zwanix, an Android application designed to help students manage their academic lives more efficiently while strengthening my software development skills.

My long-term goal is to become a professional software developer who creates innovative, high-quality software that improves people's lives. I believe learning never stops, and I am committed to continuously growing both technically and professionally while contributing meaningful solutions through technology.

"I believe technology is more than writing code—it's about solving problems, creating opportunities, and making a positive impact on people's lives."
